Wallets

A wallet with a drain to forwards everything it receives once the amount is worth the gas. One without keeps it, and withdrawals are paid from it. Balances are the token's own base units — what the chain says the address holds, and the only balance this service has. Creating one spends nothing: a wallet is funded and approved on its first real movement, not up front.

Withdraw

Only from a wallet that accumulates, and only up to what it holds less what it has already promised. A refusal here is the whole error path: once accepted, a withdrawal has no failure state. A fee becomes a second debit to the master — accepted together with the payout, but settling on its own.
